How to play

Guess the number before the fuse burns out.

  • You get 45 seconds per question, and you can guess as many times as you like.
  • After each guess you'll hear More! or Less!
  • Land within 2% of the answer to score 100 points, plus 3 for every second left. For years, only the exact year counts.
  • Out of time? Your closest guess still earns points if it was within 50%.
